SnapCompress – Privacy Policy

SnapCompress reduces photo file sizes on your device.

Data Collection

The app does not collect, store, or transmit your images. Selected photos are processed locally on your device and saved to your device storage.

The app integrates Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect device and advertising identifiers to provide and improve advertising. See Google’s advertising policies.

Permissions

  • Access to media through the system picker to let you choose photos.
  • Write access to save compressed photos.

Children’s Privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. Continued use means you accept any changes.

Contact
